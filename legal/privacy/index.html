On premotio.com

The website uses Google Analytics only if you agree in its cookie banner: it counts visits and the pages seen, with a random ID kept in a cookie, your browser and device, and an approximate location. Google Analytics does not store your IP address. Without your yes, the website sets no cookie other than the one that remembers your choice, and uses no advertising tools. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, time and page requested) for security and to keep the site working.

In the editor without an account

You can use the editor at app.premotio.com without signing in. Your projects and files then stay in your browser (IndexedDB and local storage) and are not sent to us. The browser also keeps the count of your exports for the month.

Why we may use it

  • To provide the Service you signed up for (performance of a contract): your account, content, plan, billing and connected apps.
  • Our legitimate interests: keeping the Service secure, preventing abuse (limits on sign-in codes, requests and exports), and fixing errors.
  • Your consent: Google Analytics on premotio.com, only if you say yes. You can take it back at any time under Cookie settings, at the bottom of the site.
  • Legal obligations: answering lawful requests from authorities, and the tax and accounting records Paddle keeps as Merchant of Record.

When you connect an AI app, it receives the data its permissions allow, under its maker's own privacy policy; what it has read stays with it after you disconnect it or delete your account. When an app gives the editor a link to a picture or clip, our server fetches it from that address.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and content: until you delete them or your account.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics keeps the data on each visit for 2 months; after that we see only totals.
  • Sessions: they end after 60 days without use, or when you sign out or end them in Settings.
  • Sign-in code records: up to 24 hours after the last code.
  • Records of finished exports (when each video was finished, and on which plan): about two months, enough to count the current month.
  • Frames and videos rendered for AI apps, and data-export files: 7 days.
  • Payment notifications from Paddle: 90 days.
  • Technical logs: for a limited period set by our hosting providers.
  • Deleted accounts: removed from our database and file storage at once; any copy left in a backup is gone within 30 days. Paddle keeps payment records for as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent. In the editor:

  • Copy: Settings, Storage & data, Export all data (a ZIP with your projects, presets, favourites and files). For a copy of your account details, write to us.
  • Correct: your name and photo in Settings, Account.
  • Delete: Settings, Account, Delete account.

For anything else, write to support@premotio.com; we answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU, the UK or Ukraine, you can also complain to your data protection authority (in Ukraine, the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights).

Cookies and browser storage

premotio uses only what is needed for it to work. The list is in the Cookie Policy.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in codes, session tokens and app tokens are stored only as one-way hashes. Your files are kept in private storage and reached only through signed links that expire. Database tables are closed to direct public access.

Children

premotio is not for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

We tell you by email or in the editor before a change to this policy that matters to you takes effect. The date at the top shows the current version.
